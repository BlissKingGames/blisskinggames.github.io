Drafted November 28, 2026 · Effective date to be confirmed before publication

BlissKing (블리스킹, “we”) explains how information is processed in the Android service of Find the Virus and how users can contact us about their privacy.

This policy concerns the Google Play app with package name com.BlissKing.IsolationV. We will update the policy to reflect the actual practices of other platforms before those services are released.

Four key points

  • Intended for ages 16 and older. The game is being prepared for teenagers and adults. Its intended audience is separate from its content rating and does not mean that the app verifies age.
  • Your current profile and results may be public. Other players may see your nickname, selected country, current image or avatar, grade and ranking results.
  • A personal backup saves a snapshot. It includes play records, grade progress and registered images, and normally expires after seven days. A code cannot be reused after successful restoration.
  • Uninstalling, account deletion and refunds are different. Uninstalling leaves the server account in place; account deletion does not mean a purchase refund. Request account deletion through our deletion instructions.

Information visible to other players

Your nickname, selected country, current avatar or image thumbnail, grade, rank and results may be displayed to other players in rankings and profiles.

Do not upload another person's image or personal information without permission. You may choose a built-in avatar instead of a custom image.

Selecting an image reads the chosen image and creates a game-specific copy. We do not bulk upload your entire device photo library.

Your report submissions and block list are not displayed on public leaderboards. Operators may review reported profile content and related evidence.

Local storage, backup and restoration

Detailed play history and the registered image library normally remain on your device. Current public profile information, rankings and purchase entitlements are among the information stored for the online service.

When you request a personal data backup, a snapshot of play history, progress and registered images is stored temporarily on the server. An ordinary backup code expires seven days after issue.

If a server-approved restoration remains incomplete, the retry deadline is the later of the original expiration time or 72 hours after the first restoration approval. Repeated retries do not keep extending this deadline.

Successful restoration consumes the code. Completed, expired, canceled or replaced backups become eligible for cleanup; scheduled processing and retries after failures can affect when the underlying stored data is removed.

A backup does not automatically include changes made after it was created. Authentication secrets and purchase ownership are not transferred through the device backup; the server verifies them separately.

Approving restoration on a new device revokes online access for the previous installation. This does not remotely erase the old device's local files.

Deleting the app or clearing device data without a valid backup can make device-only records and images unrecoverable. Reconnecting a server account is different from restoring a personal data backup.

Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app does not delete the server account and all related server information. Backup cancellation or expiry, profile blocking, account suspension and account deletion are separate actions.

Electronic information should be deleted in a way that makes recovery difficult; information that must be retained should be separated from information used to provide the active service.

Privacy and account deletion inquiries

You may email us about access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information, or to request account deletion. We may request proportionate identity verification to protect other users from unauthorized disclosure or deletion.

Include the game name and what you are requesting. Do not email your Google password, authentication tokens or backup restoration code.

Deleting a Google account, Google's own transaction records and refund requests are governed by the provider's separate procedures.

Submit an email request using the account and data deletion instructions. Settings → Delete account in the updated app opens the same instructions. After ownership verification and confirmation, access is blocked and deletion is processed after a seven-day grace period. We explain the schedule and any delay to the requester.

The scope includes the game authentication account, profile and thumbnail, rankings and play data, personal backups and codes, purchase-access links, and related report and block data. We also remove account data from operator recovery copies and account identifiers from management audits. Deleted records and images cannot be recovered, and reconnecting purchase access may be difficult.

Intended audience

Find the Virus is being prepared for teenagers aged 16 or older and adults. It is not being promoted primarily to young children.

This does not mean the current app verifies birth dates or automatically blocks underage access. Country-specific privacy requirements still apply, and the service should be offered only where applicable protective measures have been confirmed.

Parents and guardians may contact us about their child's information.

Security and updates

The online service uses HTTPS and checks authentication, permissions and installation access. A backup code alone is not sufficient to restore a different account's backup.

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. Changes requiring additional notice or consent will be applied after the corresponding procedure is in place.
