Request account and data deletion

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Request deletion of your Find the Virus game account and associated data from BlissKing (블리스킹). You can email us even after uninstalling the app.

Deletion request email

blisskingori@gmail.com

Subject: Find the Virus account deletion

How to request deletion

  1. Email us to request deletion of your game account and associated data. Include the game name, platform (Android), and a reply email address. You may include your game account ID if known.
  2. We explain the proportionate ownership verification steps and deletion scope to prevent another person's account from being deleted. A nickname, account ID or sender email address alone is not proof of ownership.
  3. After ownership and deletion intent are confirmed, we block access. Deletion is processed after a seven-day grace period. We notify you of completion or explain delays. Sending an email does not immediately delete the account.

Do not send Google passwords, authentication tokens, backup restoration codes or full payment details. If verification is difficult, contact us first.

Information covered

The game authentication account, associated profile and thumbnail, rankings and play data, personal backups and restoration codes, purchase-access links, and related report and block data are included. We check connected game environments, including development and production.

Account data is also removed from operator recovery copies, and account identifiers are removed from management audits. Unrelated accounts and records are preserved.

Before you confirm

  • Deleted game records and images cannot be recovered. Keep any device files you need before confirming deletion.
  • Reconnecting access to purchased products may be difficult after deletion. Deletion does not issue a purchase refund or delete your Google account.
  • Old device files are not erased remotely. Delete the relevant app data or files yourself.
  • Google transaction records and operational information separately managed by providers such as Unity and GitHub remain subject to their policies and applicable duties. We can explain separate request routes when needed.
  • If a legal retention obligation or unresolved dispute requires specific information to be retained, we explain the information, grounds and period. We also explain delays and the next processing schedule.

You can use the same email to request access, correction or restriction instead of deleting your account. Reinstalling the app is not required to submit an inquiry.
